5-fold cross-validation
What is F1–F5? The 60 runs are split into 5 folds of 12 runs. For fold k the model trains on the other 48 runs, then predicts the 12 held-out runs. The number is that fold’s RMSE in minutes: the typical size of its prediction error on trips it never saw. Lower is better. F2 holds the heavy-rain runs and F3 the VIP convoy. How to play & learning objectives

Tune the model. Pick the fastest route.

  • Underfitting (high bias): a model that is too simple, such as a straight line, misses the curve on Route A, so training and CV error both stay high.
  • Overfitting (high variance): a model that is too flexible memorises noise like the VIP convoy and flood runs. Training error falls while CV error explodes.
  • 5-fold CV: runs are split into 5 folds and each is held out once, so you see how the model behaves on trips it never trained on.
  • Sweet spot: moderate degree, some λ to damp wiggles, and α to avoid unpredictable routes.
